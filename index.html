Task 1 — Case View redesign

The analyst lives in this screen six hours a day and should always know where the case is and what to do next — the current screen answers neither, and its AI scores can't be trusted or questioned. I kept Zanko's screen and added five fixes: a workflow bar with one suggested next action, the complaint shown in full, AI conclusions that say how sure they are and take agree/disagree with a saved reason, the draft reply surfaced when it's time to respond, and a human approval before anything sends.

Task 2 — What should Zanko Reporting do next?

Three customers, one pattern: Zanko makes the reports but cannot send them, so the people became the delivery system. I built Scheduled Reports — a Schedules tab in the existing Reporting area: which report, when it goes, who gets it, and which of two versions each person gets (full report or headline). External sends wait for a one-click Approve; every send is recorded. Left for version two, on purpose: auto-send after trust is earned, picking your own sections, and loud failure handling.
